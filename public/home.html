The Nelson IT Student Association (NISA) exists to empower IT students through real-world engagement,
interdisciplinary collaboration, and career-aligned development opportunities.
We aim to cultivate a supportive community that bridges the gap between education and industry,
inspires innovation, and amplifies student voices through events, projects, and mentorship.
